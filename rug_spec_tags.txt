RUG (HALI / KILIM) TAGS THAT MATTER FOR LOW MAINTENANCE
=======================================================

The one preference: UPKEEP IS PRIORITY NUMBER ONE. Wash it as rarely as possible, and a
stain must not ruin it.

This list was purged down from 105 tags to only the ones that affect that. Everything
else (brand, size, price, style, theme, certificates, delivery, sorting, and the
comfort and safety flags) has been removed. It is not a filter yet, and can be added back later.

Values marked GOOD or AVOID are my reading of how the material or construction behaves,
not a lab result. Check the label and the reviews on the actual product.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. STAIN AND DIRT (the biggest effect on upkeep)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Ana malzeme / elyaf (main material / fibre)
   GOOD:  Polipropilen / PP, Heatset polipropilen (does not absorb liquid, so stains sit
          on top and wipe off), Polyester
   OKAY:  Naylon / poliamid (tough, but takes up coffee, wine and dye stains more easily)
   AVOID: Viskon / bambu ipeği (water marks and yellowing, hard to clean), Yün (absorbs
          liquid, sheds), Jüt / sisal / deniz otu / hasır (stain permanently and cannot
          be wet-cleaned), Pamuk (stains easily), Şönil (snags and stains), Suni kürk,
          Deri / kürk, Ipek

2. Karışım oranı (blend percentage): pure synthetic is easier to clean than a blend with
   natural fibre

3. İplik türü ve kalınlığı (yarn type): Heatset, BCF, Frize (frisé). Tight, hard-twist
   yarn holds less dirt

4. Leke tutmaz / leke itici (stain resistant / stain repellent): yes

5. Su geçirmez / su itici (waterproof / water repellent): yes

6. Toz tutmaz / az toz tutar (low dust): yes

7. Tüy dökmez / dökmez (non-shedding): yes. Shedding means vacuuming forever

8. Antistatik (antistatic): yes. Static pulls dust and hair onto the rug

9. Renk (colour), Ton (tone), Renk sayısı (number of colours), Desen yoğunluğu
   (pattern busyness): these decide how much a stain SHOWS
   GOOD:  Koyu tonlar, Orta tonlar, Kahverengi, Gri, Antrasit, Vizon, Çok renkli,
          Yoğun desen (busy pattern), Benekli, Melanj
   AVOID: Krem, Ekru, Beyaz, Açık bej, Düz tek renk (show every mark)

10. Doku (texture) and Yüzey (finish): Mat, Dokulu are GOOD. Parlak surfaces (viscose
    and similar) show every footprint


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. CONSTRUCTION THAT TRAPS OR SHOWS DIRT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. Ürün tipi (product type)
    GOOD:  Kilim, Düz dokuma halı (flat weave rug), Makine halısı, Dış mekan halısı
           (outdoor rugs are made to be hosed down and tend to be polypropylene flatweave)
    AVOID: Shaggy, Jüt halı, Şönil halı, Suni kürk halı, Patchwork, Kürk halı

12. Hav yüksekliği (pile height)
    GOOD:  Düz dokuma / hav yok, Çok kısa havlı (3 mm or less), Kısa havlı (up to 8 mm)
    AVOID: Uzun havlı, Shaggy (15 mm and up): crumbs and hair sink in and stains soak down

13. Hav tipi (pile type): Kesik hav (cut pile) is usually easier to vacuum than
    İlmekli / lup hav (loop pile), which can snag

14. Hav yoğunluğu (pile density) and Dokuma yoğunluğu / nokta sayısı (weave density):
    higher is GOOD. Dense construction gives dirt and liquid less room to get in

15. Dokuma tipi / üretim yöntemi (weave type / how it is made)
    GOOD:  Makine dokuma (machine woven), Düz dokuma kilim (flatweave), Jakarlı (jacquard)
    AVOID: El dokuma / el yapımı / tufting (hand tufted rugs shed, and the glued backing
           can fail when cleaned or washed)

16. Baskı tipi (print type): woven pattern is GOOD, because a printed pattern can fade
    when you clean or wash it

17. Aşınma sınıfı / kalite sınıfı (wear class / quality class): higher is GOOD

18. Kullanım alanı / oda (room / use) and Trafik yoğunluğu (traffic level)
    GOOD:  Mutfak, Antre / giriş, Koridor, Yoğun trafik (made for dirt and spills)

19. İç mekan / dış mekan (indoor / outdoor): Outdoor is worth searching too, because
    they are designed for water, dirt and UV

20. Ürün ömrü (expected life): longer is GOOD


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. EDGES AND SIDES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
21. Kenar işlemesi (edge finish): Overlok (overlocked) or Bantlı is GOOD, because it will
    not fray. Kesik kenar (raw cut edge) is AVOID
22. Saçak (fringe): Saçaksız (no fringe) is GOOD. Fringe traps crumbs and hair, and
    tangles in a vacuum
23. Çift taraflı kullanım (reversible): yes. When one side is stained, flip it


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. CLEANING AND WASHING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
24. Yıkanabilir / makinede yıkanabilir (washable / machine washable): yes. You want to
    wash it rarely, but this is the rescue when something goes badly wrong
25. Yıkama talimatı ve derecesi (wash instruction and temperature): 30 degrees, cold
    wash, no special handling is GOOD
26. Kurutma (drying): line dry or tumble dry OK. AVOID "kuru temizleme" (dry clean only)
27. Çabuk kurur (quick drying): yes. A damp rug leads to mould and smell
28. Bakım kolaylığı (ease of care): Kolay (easy)
29. Yıkama sonrası çekme (shrinkage after washing): Çekmez (does not shrink)
30. Renk haslığı (colour fastness): high, or Solmaz (does not fade). Cleaning agents and
    washing must not bleed the colours
31. Boncuklanma / tüylenme (pilling / fuzzing): Yok (none). Pilling looks worn and needs
    trimming
32. Robot süpürge uyumlu (robot vacuum compatible): yes


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. VINYL FLOOR SAFETY (a hard rule for this floor, not an upkeep preference)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
33. Taban malzemesi / tipi (backing material / type)
    GOOD:  Keçe taban (felt), Pamuk taban, Backing that says it is safe for PVC / vinyl
    AVOID: Lateks, Kauçuk, PVC noktalı taban (latex, rubber and PVC-dotted backing can
           stain a vinyl floor yellow or brown)
34. Kaymaz (non-slip) and Uygun halı altlığı (compatible rug pad): the pad must also be
    safe for vinyl

    Note: many washable rugs use a latex or rubber backing. That is a direct clash with
    the vinyl floor. Look for a washable rug with a felt or fabric back, or one that
    needs no non-slip backing.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F. REAL-LIFE EVIDENCE (not a product attribute, but the best test of upkeep claims)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
35. Ürün puanı ve yorum sayısı (product rating and number of reviews)
36. Fotoğraflı yorum (reviews with photos): look for photos taken months later, and read
    the reviews that mention stains, washing, shedding and colour fading
