Everything the room design has settled on but not bought yet, pulled from `PROJECT.md`. No links, so search terms are given
instead. **Status: move-in is at least a month away — do not shop until about a week or two before moving in**, except where

## 1. Flooring (rolled vinyl over the tile)

Budget has no ceiling for this item only (dropped 2026-09-26). Everything else below still has no fixed budget stated, but
treat it normally, not as unlimited.

| # | Item | Search term | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vinyl sheet (muşamba) | `parke desenli PVC muşamba` | Colour: dark walnut (`koyu kahve` / `ceviz desenli`). 2.5–3 mm thick, **matte** (never glossy), foam- or felt-backed, wear layer 0.3 mm or more. **3 m wide roll**, about **15 m²** (3 m × ~5 m, covers the main room and the hall with trimming waste, no seam) |
| 2 | Double-sided tape | `çift taraflı halı bandı` | Holds the edges to the tile; no glue |
| 3 | Utility knife + spare blades | `maket bıçağı` | Blades dull fast; a dull blade tears the sheet |
| 4 | Long metal ruler | `metal cetvel` | For straight cuts |
| 5 | Felt pads, furniture legs | `keçe ayak pedi` | Every leg in the room, wide ones under heavy pieces. Soft vinyl dents permanently otherwise |
| 6 | Clear silicone (optional) | `şeffaf silikon` | One thin line at the bottom of the walls, especially by the sink |
| 7 | Degreaser (only if the tile is greasy) | `yağ çözücü` | Tape will not stick to a dirty tile |
| 8 | Grout filler (only if grout lines are deeper than ~2 mm) | `derz dolgusu` | Check with a fingernail first |

**Before ordering:** tape-measure the real room width (derived at ~2.71 m; confirm it), check the gap under the entrance
door (the vinyl adds ~3 mm), and check the roll's tube length fits the stairwell or lift.

## 3. Daybed / seating hero

### 3a. Kilim (the firming layer, doubles as the frame's front cover)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Flat-woven kilim | Alpina Home "Stella Jüt Hasır" or equivalent: polypropylene, woven backing, machine-made, thin flat weave (no pile, no foam), beige, no fringe. Reference price seen: ~1,390 TL |

**⚠️ Size conflict, not resolved — read before ordering.** The kilim needs to cover the mattress top (90 cm) plus drape
down the *entire* visible front face of the bed, which now reaches all the way to the top of the frame's legs, not just the
mattress edge. That drop is 39 cm (17 cm mattress + 22 cm white board), so the kilim needs **about 129 cm across its
width** (90 + 39). The 110 × 190 cm size looked at earlier is **19 cm short** of that — it will cover the top and most of
the front, but stop above the legs and leave the white board's bottom edge showing. Pick one:
- Find a wider kilim (roughly 130–140 cm across) in the same look, or
- Keep the 110 cm kilim and cover the remaining strip of white board with something else (paint, a fabric skirt, trim), or
- Accept the board showing.
This is a decision for a person, not something to guess at — see [tools/add_daybed.py](tools/add_daybed.py) decision note.

### 3e. Arm-rest boxes — commission, not off-the-shelf

Plan is a carpenter builds the box, strapped to the bed frame's legs (item 3d). Give the carpenter:

| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| Quantity | 2, identical |
| Size | 30 cm deep (along the wall) × 90 cm wide × 70 cm tall |
| Shape | Hollow, recessed plinth base, plain (not box-jointed — it's getting covered, see below) lift-off lid with rounded top corners and a small rounded finger-notch to lift it (**no handle**) |
| Interior | Roughly 88 × 24 × 64 cm clear, big enough for a pillow or a folded blanket |
| Finish under the cover | Plain is fine — box-joint corners were dropped since a slipcover hides them anyway. Leave the plinth (the recessed base) as bare, finished wood; it peeks out under the cover |

### 3f. Arm-rest slipcover — **finish decided** (fitted beige bouclé)

Chosen over three other directions (a loose wrapped-and-strapped throw, glued/stapled upholstery, a padded body with a separate cushion lid) because it's the most finished-looking and, unlike glued upholstery, stays removable and washable.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fitted cover × 2 | Wraps the box body and lid, sized to the 30×90×70 cm boxes above. A seam (zip or Velcro) runs along the lid line so the lid still lifts off underneath |
| Search terms | `ölçüye özel kılıf dikimi` (tailor-made to size), `sandık kılıfı` / `kutu kılıfı`, `fermuarlı kılıf` (zip) or `cırt bantlı kılıf` (Velcro) |
| Fabric | Beige bouclé — `boucle kumaş` (or `kalın keten kumaş`, heavy linen, as a fallback), colour bej |
| Padding | A thin layer between the wood and the fabric for actual softness, not just a padded look — `ince sünger vatka` / `dolgu vatka` |
| Alternative sourcing | An upholsterer (`döşemeci`) can fit and staple the cover directly instead of a separate sewn slipcover, if a removable one isn't needed |
